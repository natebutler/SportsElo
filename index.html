💡 How to Read Elo Ratings & Win Probabilities

Elo is a dynamic ranking system where 1500 represents an average team. Whenever two teams play, the rating difference calculates the expected outcome. Beating a higher-rated opponent awards more points than beating an underdog.

Even Matchup
0 pt difference

Both teams have an exact 50% chance to win.

Moderate Favorite
+100 pt difference

The favorite has a ~64% chance to win (e.g. 1600 vs. 1500).

Heavy Favorite
+200 pt difference

The favorite has a ~76% chance to win (e.g. 1650 vs. 1450).
